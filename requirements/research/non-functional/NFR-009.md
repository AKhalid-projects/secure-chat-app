# NFR-009: Native capability declarations

The Secure Chat Application shall declare platform permissions, background execution modes, and edge-to-edge user interface settings required for camera, microphone, notifications, calls, and configuration screens in iOS and Android manifests via Expo configuration. Category: **compatibility**; priority: **High**. Mobile platforms gate sensitive capabilities through declared intents; the requirement captures that **policy surface** as a non-functional obligation.

Evidence is concentrated in `app.json` with permission lists and background modes. Confidence is **explicit**. Verification includes store policy checks at a high level and runtime permission prompts on device.

**FR-005**, **FR-014**, and **FR-016** depend on these declarations; **NFR-001** depends on successful native builds.
