# NFR-011: Third-party service integration

The Secure Chat Application shall depend on CometChat cloud services for messaging and calling semantics and on Firebase Cloud Messaging (via React Native Firebase) for push transport on Android, with Apple push services on iOS, such that availability and behaviour of those services bound application capabilities. Category: **integrability**; priority: **High**. The requirement makes explicit that the client is a **thin layer** over external providers rather than a self-contained system.

Evidence appears in dependency declarations and integration imports throughout messaging, calls, and notification modules. Confidence is **explicit**. Verification must acknowledge external outages and quotas as out-of-repo risks.

**FR-001**, **FR-015**, **FR-016**, and **NFR-008** are closely related.
